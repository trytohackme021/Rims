Phase 1: Curated Census Dataset & Metadata Schema

Initial Baseline Prompt:
Create a 300-line CSV dataset of library books and journals for a management college with departments, circulation, downloads, cost, and satisfaction scores, plus a data dictionary.
Production-Grade Refined Prompt:
Act as a Senior Academic Data Architect conducting an institutional collection census for Rajasthan Institute of Management & Sciences (RIMS). Generate a statistically coherent, realistic audit sample of exactly 300 records reflecting an annual expenditure of ₹18.00 Lakh facing a mandated 30% reduction (ceiling: ₹12.60 Lakh). Required Specifications: 1. record_id: Discrete alphanumeric format ('ACAD-LIB-1001' to 'ACAD-LIB-1300') 2. department: Distribution across ['School of Business', 'School of Technology & Computing', 'School of Commerce & Finance', 'Department of Social Sciences', 'Department of Pure & Applied Sciences'] 3. resource_type: ['book', 'journal', 'e-resource'] with ~58% monographs, 27% serials, 15% digital bundles 4. subject_category: Realistic accredited course domains per school 5. publication_year: Monographs spanning 1997–2024; serials 2016–2024 6. times_issued_last_year: Explicitly model the vintage threshold (pre-2015 titles circulate <1.4 times; 2021-2024 editions circulate 38-64 times) 7. downloads_last_year: Reflect serial dormancy (57% of journals must log <10 downloads/yr) 8. annual_cost: INR figures reflecting academic procurement (Books: ₹520–₹3,450; Journals: ₹28,500–₹89,000; E-resources: ₹14,500–₹46,000) 9. visit_time_slot: Four operational intervals ['Slot A: 09:00 - 11:30', 'Slot B: 11:30 - 13:30', 'Slot C: 13:30 - 15:30', 'Slot D: 15:30 - 17:00'], capturing the late afternoon lab conflict 10. student_year: ['Year 1 (Freshman UG)' through 'Master Level (PG-2)'] 11. satisfaction_score: Integer Likert 1-5 correlated with material currency and cohort seniority Also construct a comprehensive markdown Data Dictionary detailing field domains, constraints, and audit notes.
Analytical Adjustments & Rationale:
  • School-Level Operational Grounding: Replaced generic departments with distinct Schools and introduced explicit correlations linking edition vintage to circulation velocity.
  • Seeded Determinism: Utilized deterministic pseudo-random seeding (Seed: 888) to guarantee reproducible audit metrics that strictly reconcile with institutional budget totals.
