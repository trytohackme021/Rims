# RIMS Knowledge Commons Campaign: Creative Strategy & Canva AI Guide
## Multi-Channel Patron Engagement Collateral (Editorial Bauhaus Aesthetic)

**Consultancy Partner:** Apex Higher Education Advisory Partners (Apex HEAP)  
**Client:** Rajasthan Institute of Management & Sciences (RIMS)  
**Core Campaign Objective:** Reverse footfall decline by directly communicating the extended 9:00 PM hours, the Zero-Waitlist 2024 Course Reserve Vault, and newly opened collaborative learning pods.

## Deliverable 4A: Instagram Square Post (1080 × 1080 px)

### Strategic Message & Data Benchmark
- **Primary Display Headline:** `YOUR SYLLABUS. FRESH OFF THE PRESS.`
- **Sub-headline:** `Stop studying from 2005 editions. We updated our collection and extended the doors.`
- **Verified Audit Data Point:**  
  `"42% of older holdings averaged under 1.4 checkouts. We stocked 2024 syllabus editions across all 5 schools and opened until 9:00 PM!"`
- **Call-to-Action (CTA):**  
  `"Claim your study pod tonight! Mon–Fri: 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM • Link in bio to reserve books"`
