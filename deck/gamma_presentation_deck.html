Slide 3 / 10 • Audit Finding 1

Finding #1: Imprint Obsolescence & The Vintage Cliff

The 2015 Currency Cliff

42% of holdings are pre-2015, averaging merely 1.34 checkouts/year (58 titles had 0 checkouts). In stark contrast, 2021–2024 editions circulate 38–64 times/year. Students read eagerly when books match active exam syllabi.

School Disparity

Business & Computing drive 71.4% of institutional engagement. Social Sciences and Pure Sciences remain completely detached due to 74% obsolete collections and zero licensed e-packages.

Slide 5 / 10 • Audit Finding 3

Finding #3: The 17:00 Timetable Lockout

Diurnal Footfall Profile

• Slot A (09:00 - 11:30): 33.0% (Morning study gap)

• Slot B (11:30 - 13:30): 39.0% (Midday peak rush)

• Slot C (13:30 - 15:30): 18.0%

• Slot D (15:30 - 17:00): 10.0% (74% footfall collapse)

Root Cause: Lab Scheduling

84% of senior undergraduate laboratory practicals and workshops run until 16:30. Closing doors at 17:00 shuts out 2,200 day scholars just as their academic day finishes. Extending hours to 21:00 is essential.
