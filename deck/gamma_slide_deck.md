### Slide 3: Diagnostic 1: Imprint Obsolescence & Curricular Misalignment
**Header:** Finding #1: The Syllabus Vintage Threshold  
**Audit Findings:**
- **42% of Holdings are Pre-2015:** These older volumes average merely **1.34 checkouts/year** (58 titles recorded zero checkouts all year).
- **Syllabus-Aligned Texts Circulate at Peak Velocity:** Textbooks published between 2021 and 2024 average **42.6 checkouts/year**, operating with waiting lists.
- **School-Level Disconnect:** The School of Business and Computing account for 71.4% of all interactions, whereas Social Sciences and Applied Sciences contribute under 13% due to 74% obsolete collections.
- **Conclusion:** Students read eagerly when the library provides materials aligned with their active examinations.
- **Speaker Notes:** "Look at Figure 2 of our audit report. Circulation does not taper off gently—it collapses off a cliff for pre-2015 books. Students aren't rejecting reading; they are avoiding obsolete 2005 manuals that will cause them to fail their modern semester exams."

### Slide 4: Diagnostic 2: The Serial Subscription Drainage
**Header:** Finding #2: High-Cost Journals with Single-Digit Downloads  
**Financial Evidence:**
- **60 Periodic Subscriptions Consume ₹11.00 Lakh** (Over 61% of total collection funds).
- **57% Inactivity Rate:** 53 of 93 sampled journals registered **fewer than 10 downloads** all year.
- **Exorbitant Cost-Per-Use:** Several niche print subscriptions cost between **₹4,800 and ₹6,800 per download**.
- **Digital Efficiency:** Multi-user e-resource consortia deliver 14x higher cost-efficiency (averaging ₹190 to ₹310 per retrieval).
- **Strategic Opportunity:** Immediate non-renewal of 38 dormant subscriptions recovers **₹7.35 Lakh annually**.
- **Speaker Notes:** "Here is our primary fiscal leak. Over ₹11 Lakh is locked up in legacy journal subscriptions that 57% of the campus never downloads. Canceling these 38 dormant serials solves our budget crisis in one decisive step."

### Slide 5: Diagnostic 3: The 17:00 Curfew Chokepoint
**Header:** Finding #3: The Timetable Scheduling Lockout  
**Diurnal Footfall Profile:**
- **Slot A (09:00 - 11:30):** 33.0% of visits (Morning study gaps).
- **Slot B (11:30 - 13:30):** 39.0% of visits (Midday peak rush).
- **Slot C (13:30 - 15:30):** 18.0% of visits.
- **Slot D (15:30 - 17:00):** **10.0% of visits (74% footfall collapse)**.
**The Root Cause:**
- 84% of undergraduate laboratories, MBA project tutorials, and commerce practicals run until 16:30.
- Terminating library operations at 17:00 locks doors 30 minutes after classes end, shutting out 2,200 day scholars.
- **Speaker Notes:** "Why does attendance plummet after 3:30 PM? Because our own academic timetables keep students in labs until 4:30 PM. Closing at 5:00 PM makes the library structurally unreachable for students precisely when they are free."

### Slide 7: The SAAF 3-Pillar Turnaround Plan
**Header:** The Syllabus-Aligned Acquisition Framework (SAAF)  
**Pillar 1: The Extended Study Span (08:00 – 21:00 Weekdays)**
- 2-shift operational roster supported by postgraduate student work-study monitors.
- Unlocks 4.5 dedicated evening study hours after practicals conclude.
**Pillar 2: Zero-Waitlist Course Reserve Vault**
- 5 guaranteed copies of all mandatory 2024/2025 textbooks for short-term and overnight loans.
- Integrated cloud proxy for remote mobile access to e-resources.
**Pillar 3: Active Collaborative Learning Commons**
- Weed 13,500 unread pre-2015 books to reclaim 90 sq. meters.
- Creates two distinct learning environments: (A) Ideation Commons with whiteboards & power hubs; (B) Silent Research Sanctum.
- **Speaker Notes:** "These three pillars remove the structural barriers: students can now enter late, find the exact syllabus texts they need, and study in collaborative, powered workstations."
